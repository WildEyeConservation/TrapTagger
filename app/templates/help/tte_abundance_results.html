Time-to-event abundance results

The results table summarises the time-to-event (TTE) abundance estimate for the selected species from the R spaceNtime package (tte_estN_fn()).

Metrics include:

  • Abundance (N): Estimated population size in the study area.
  • Standard error: Standard error of the abundance estimate.
  • Lower CI / Upper CI: Lower and upper bounds of the log-normal confidence interval for abundance.
  • Detections: Number of detections of the selected species used in the analysis.
  • Cameras: Number of camera sites included.
  • Sampling occasions: Number of sampling occasions constructed for the TTE analysis.
  • Study area (km²): Study area used for abundance estimation (entered in km²; converted to m² for tte_estN_fn()).
  • Viewable area used (m²): Camera viewshed area used by the model. Either the value you entered directly, or the (possibly edited) area generated from field of view and effective detection distance.
  • Species speed (m/hr): Average movement speed used to set TTE sampling period length via tte_samp_per().
  • TTE sampling period (s): Length of each time-to-event sampling period in seconds, derived from viewable area and species speed.
  • Area mode: Whether viewable area was entered directly or derived from camera field of view.
  • Camera field of view (°): Horizontal field of view used when area mode is derived from FOV.
  • Effective detection radius (m): When FOV mode is used, the effective detection distance fitted to detection distances and used with camera field of view (theta) to derive viewable area.
