Distance sampling model selection

Detection-function models are selected automatically following the camera-trap distance sampling approach (Howe et al. 2019). Candidate models are fitted across three key-function families, then compared in two stages.

The Model selection tab shows:

  • QAIC — uniform key: Quasi-AIC comparisons among adjustment-term options within the uniform key-function family. Lower QAIC is better within the family.
  • QAIC — half-normal key: The same within-family QAIC comparison for half-normal key functions.
  • QAIC — hazard-rate key: The same within-family QAIC comparison for hazard-rate key functions.
  • Final model comparison (χ²/df): The QAIC winner from each key-function family is compared using the chi-square goodness-of-fit statistic divided by degrees of freedom (chi2_select). The model with the lowest χ²/df is selected for density estimation.

Typical columns in these tables include model name or formula, QAIC (or ΔQAIC), χ², degrees of freedom, and χ²/df. Exact columns depend on what the fitted models return for that run.

The selected model name, family, selection method, and overdispersion (χ²/df) are also summarised on the Estimates tab. Notes or warnings from model selection, if any, appear under the estimates table.
