Time-to-event abundance

Time-to-event (TTE) abundance estimates population size (N) for a single species from camera-trap detections using the R spaceNtime package (Moeller et al. 2018).

Key requirements:

  • One species: Select a single species for each run.
  • Study area: Enter the survey area in m² passed to tte_estN_fn().
  • Viewable area: Enter the camera viewshed area in m² directly, or choose field-of-view mode to derive effective detection radius (95th percentile of detection distances) and sector area from FOV.
  • Species speed: Average movement speed in m/hr used by tte_samp_per() to set TTE sampling period length. Leave blank for the default 30 m/hr.
  • Detections: At least one detection of the selected species is required. Field-of-view mode additionally requires measured detection distances.

Advanced settings:

  • Periods per occasion (nper): Number of TTE sampling periods in each occasion (default 24).
  • Time between occasions: Gap between sampling occasions allowing animals to re-randomize (default 2 hours).

Results report abundance (N), standard error, and log-normal confidence intervals from tte_estN_fn(), along with the viewable area, species speed, and sampling settings used. Download CSV exports for detections and deployment tables with Download CSV.
